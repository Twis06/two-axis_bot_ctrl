## 4. Stress set (Simulated; §4 of the numbers file; seeds 1–3; baseline and plan)

- **Yaw plan mismatch.**
  - The baseline measures yaw and is barely affected: C at 25 ms late ×0.85 is tracked 3/3 with path RMS 0.38°.
  - The plan mode is fed the wrong yaw, and its path RMS is three times higher on the same case (1.26° against 0.38°). Its peak error rises to 3.4°.

- **Asymmetric loss.** This is emulated: each case drops exactly the stated share of messages in one direction, using ±0.25 ms blackout windows, because `sim.config` has no per-direction drop probability.

## 5. Faulted D/E Monte Carlo trials, classified (Simulated; torque terms Calculated)

11 of the 40 trials fault: D 5/20 and E 6/20, as in Task 2. None was excluded.

**Rule (project assumption):** the cause is the largest term the host does not model, averaged over the 100 ms before the first trip. The candidate terms are payload, gravity-model error, coupling prediction error, inertia error, Kt error and, added in review round 1, Coulomb-friction error, viscous-damping error and the bounded disturbance d(t) — every randomised quantity. A trial is labelled *physically infeasible* if its static payload torque τ_lat alone exceeds the true actuator capacity, otherwise *limit-bound* if the command was at the current limit for at least 50 % of that window.

**Result:**
- **All 11 are attributed to unmodelled payload torque** (0.19–0.34 N·m); no added term changes an attribution.
- **E00 and E14 are physically infeasible:** their lateral payload torque exceeds Kt·2.4 A even at rest. E11, E16 and E17 are limit-bound.
- Coupling prediction error is zero in every trial, because D/E hold yaw still.
- 9 of 11 trip within 0.4 s of the start. D09 trips at 5.3 s and E16 at 1.57 s.

**Outcome:** every faulted trial is caught and held with the request suspended. There is no lockout in this set. Suspended time is 72–95 % of the run, or 11 % for D09, which tripped late.

**Interpretation (Calculated from the logged terms, not a hardware finding).** The rule **attributes; it does not discriminate.** Payload dominates by construction in payload scenarios (every D/E trial carries 0.3–1.0 kg), and 6 non-faulted trials carry payloads in the faulted range (0.73–0.83 kg: D00, D06, D11, D13, D17, E15). The evidence supports "when these trials tripped, the unmodelled payload torque was the largest unmodelled term"; it does not show *why* a trial trips and a similar one does not. That the payload is the leading term is consistent with Task 3's question (load estimation).

## 8. Remaining limitations and decision items for the coordinator

**Decision items.** No controller change was made; each would be a post-freeze change.
1. **Loaded fallback: a current-limited stopping distance.**
   - On D at +35 mm, a 100 ms command-only or both-direction outage produced up to 42° of motion during about 150 ms of drive fallback, entered at about −9.6 rad/s. The excursion is **mostly set by the drive's fallback damping gain** (0.05 N·m·s/rad: 38.8°; 0.15: 30.3°; 1.0: 29.0°, diagnostic sweep), **bounded below at ≈ 29° by the 3.2 A current limit** (binding 71 % of the span at c = 1.0), and worsened by the +35 mm payload torque acting along the motion. With the payload at −35 mm the same outage moves the axis at most 14.4°. (Round 0 blamed weak damping alone and round 1 blamed the current limit alone; the sweep shows both, with damping dominant at the frozen gain.)
   - Per plan §2, fallback is motion reduction, not a hold, so this is not a contract violation.
2. **Suspend-and-hold vs keep-pushing after a physical overload (INF-P).**
   - The baseline holds ≈ 49° (47–51°) from the current request (0°), at 1.1–1.4 A. Legacy ends ≈ 21–24° from it, at the 2.4 A limit 98.8 % of the time.
   - The baseline's behaviour matches its stated contract: the trip is disclosed and the request suspended until replan. Legacy's does not.
   - Confirm that this is the intended trade-off.
3. **Yaw information mode.**
   - The estimate-mode baseline pays 3–9 % of net progress on C-family cases through the yaw shrink, and loses the 4A tracked verdict in the C corner and in C with yaw 10 ms late ×1.10.
   - The plan mode is worse when the yaw deviates from its plan (the 25 ms ×0.85 case) and at 2× coupling.
   - Recommendation: keep the estimate as the claimed baseline; the plan mode stays optional.
4. **Over-tracking** (§7): gain > 1.10 over 2.97–14.6 Hz, peak 1.27 at 6.7 Hz. Declare it as an envelope limit, or retune in a later design comparison (Packet 4C). Not changed here.
5. **Proposed interface change (sim owner):** a per-direction drop probability in `TimingConfig`, so asymmetric loss can be random rather than emulated with windows.

**Limitations of this evidence:**
- **Sampled, not proven.** Monte Carlo and the stress cases are sampled evidence over chosen, uncalibrated ranges (seeds 1–3 or 1–5).
- **Legacy is a reconstruction** fitted in Phase 1, not the hardware controller.
- **The fault-cause rule is post hoc.** It uses the true plant state and parameters, so it is a diagnostic, never something the controller could know. A trial with two comparable terms would get only one label; none of the 11 was close (payload was at least 2.3× the next term).
- **Two diagnostic comparators are not designs:** the bench variant (friction removed in plant and host), and `use_governor=False` in the saturation step re-runs.
- **Unmet performance goals, with their delivered-motion trade-off:**
  - D and E are not tracked; they deliver 83 % and 51 % of the path time, with governed RMS 4.1–4.25°.
  - M2 is not completed at 0.7 kg and 2.4 A.
  - Command-only loss of 30 % leaves 39–50 % of the path delivered.
  - All of these are declared; none is scored as success.
- **Figure scope:** figures show seed 1 (or seed 1 of the timeline case). The tables carry the spread.

### Coordinator decisions on the items above (planner, 2026-09-24; revised after review round 1)

1. **Loaded fallback:** *accepted and declared.* The stopping distance is mostly set by the drive's fallback damping gain, bounded below (≈ 29° here) by the current limit, and worsened by unmodelled payload torque. **Proposed** for the qualification plan: a stronger drive fallback damping gain (hardware/qualification item; not the frozen baseline), and beyond the ≈ 29° current-limited floor a mechanical brake or a reduced speed/position envelope under unknown payload.
2. **Physically infeasible load:** *confirmed* suspend-and-hold contract. The corrected distances are reported: ≈ 49° (baseline) against ≈ 24° (legacy), while legacy saturates 98.8 % of the time.
3. **Yaw information mode.** *The causal estimate remains the claimed baseline.* The plan look-ahead remains an optional, labelled mode whose assumption (yaw follows its plan) must be verified on hardware.
4. **Over-tracking:** *declared* as a Calculated property of the frozen loop: gain > 1.05 over 2.26–17.7 Hz and > 1.10 over 2.97–14.6 Hz, peak ≈ 1.27 at 6.7 Hz. The Task 2 requests lie at its lower edge (2.2 Hz gain ≈ 1.05). Revisit in 4C.

The proposed per-direction drop probability in `TimingConfig` is noted for the simulator owner. Blackout-share emulation is accepted for 4B and declared.
