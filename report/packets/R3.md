## Limitations

- **Missing challenge:** the payload-change challenge cannot run without a simulator change. That would alter the frozen baseline's sources, so it was not made, and the gate records it as `incomplete`.
- **Mostly unexercised:** challenge coverage of the *active* correction is thin, because the exact candidate is rarely usable. Most challenges test the candidate while it is abstaining.
- **"Stale-result application" is checked indirectly,** as "no correction while unusable". The estimator's internal rejection of late worker results is covered by the L1/L2 tests, not by closed-loop logs.
- **Assumed thresholds:** the gate thresholds (10%, 80%, 20%, progress slack 0.01) are the registered project thresholds, and the progress slack is a declared implementation choice.
